Meet Piggy Boba

Say hello to Piggy Boba, the roly-poly little piggy named after everyone's favorite bubble tea treat! Just like a fresh cup of boba, Piggy Boba is soft, squishy, and impossible to resist. She waddles around with her round little belly bouncing with every step, always on the hunt for her next snack.

Piggy Boba loves lounging in cozy spots, snuffling through blankets, and giving the best snorty greetings to anyone who comes by. Her chubby cheeks and tiny trotters make her look like she was made for cuddles, and her playful, curious nature means there's never a dull moment when she's around.
