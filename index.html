Meet Lulu

Say hello to Lulu, the pint-sized piggy with a giant personality! Lulu spends her days rooting around the yard, flopping over for belly rubs, and grunting happily whenever snack time rolls around. With her curly tail, twitchy little snout, and irresistibly floppy ears, Lulu has a way of making everyone smile the moment they meet her.

Whether she's zooming across the grass at full speed, taking a mud bath, or curling up for a nap in a cozy blanket nest, Lulu proves that pigs are some of the smartest, sweetest, and most entertaining animals around. She loves belly scratches, fresh veggies, and being the center of attention — and honestly, who could blame her?
